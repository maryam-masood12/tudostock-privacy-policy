Account Deletion Request

TuDoStock Book

You can request deletion of your TuDoStock Book account and associated business data without accessing the app.

How to request deletion

Email us from your registered email address using the contact details below. Use the subject Account Deletion Request.

Email: tudostocksupport@gmail.com

Please include your registered email address.

Verification and deletion

We will verify account ownership before processing a request. Never send your password or verification codes by email.

Once verified, the request will be handled according to our account deletion process and applicable legal requirements. Any data that must be retained by law will be handled as required.
