Your business. Your records. Your control.

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TuDoStock Book ("TuDoStock", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application. TuDoStock Book helps businesses manage inventory, customers, suppliers, transactions, employees, and business records.

By using the app, you acknowledge that your information may be processed as described in this policy. Please do not enter information that you are not authorized to manage or share.

1. Information We Handle

The information processed by TuDoStock Book depends on the features you use and the information you choose to enter.

Account Information

  • Email address and account identifiers used for sign-in.
  • Account and profile information that you provide.

Business and Shop Information

  • Shop name and business details that you enter.
  • Business preferences, including selected language and currency.

Customer and Supplier Information

  • Names, phone numbers, account balances, and related details you enter.
  • Records associated with customer and supplier account activity.

Inventory and Transaction Information

  • Products, categories, quantities, stock movements, and purchase costs.
  • Sales, purchases, collections, payments, and related transaction notes.
  • Reports and records generated from information maintained in the app.
  • Product images that you choose to upload.

Employee and Sharing Information

  • Employee details, assigned roles, permissions, and salary records where entered.
  • Records of supported business changes and transaction activity.
  • Information needed to establish and manage shared customer or supplier accounts.

2. How We Use Information

We use information as needed to provide the app's features, including to:

  • Create and manage user accounts and business profiles.
  • Maintain inventory, customer and supplier accounts, and transaction records.
  • Calculate and display business balances, summaries, and reports.
  • Manage employee access and permissions.
  • Enable account sharing when a business owner chooses to share an account.
  • Store and synchronize supported business information across signed-in devices.
  • Provide account security, troubleshoot issues, and support app operation.
  • Respond to questions or requests that you send to us.

3. Cloud Storage and Third-Party Services

TuDoStock Book uses Google Firebase services to support features such as authentication, cloud database storage, and product image storage. Depending on the feature, information may be processed using:

  • Firebase Authentication: to support account sign-in.
  • Cloud Firestore: to store and synchronize supported business and account records.
  • Firebase Storage: to store product images and other supported uploaded files.

These service providers process information as needed to provide their services and subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices. Information may be processed on servers located outside your country.

You can review Google's privacy information at Google Privacy Policy and Firebase information at Firebase Privacy and Security.

4. Who Can Access Business Information?

Business Owners

A business owner can manage the business information and records associated with their shop through the features available in the app.

Employees

Employees may access business information according to the roles and permissions assigned to their accounts. Business owners are responsible for reviewing and managing these permissions.

Shared Customer and Supplier Accounts

When an owner shares a customer or supplier account, the recipient may view the information made available through that sharing relationship. Share accounts only with people you trust and review access when needed.

Service Providers and Legal Requirements

Information may be processed by the infrastructure providers described above. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect the security and integrity of the app, subject to applicable legal requirements.

5. Data Sharing and Selling

TuDoStock's account-sharing feature allows business owners to provide access to selected customer or supplier account information. This sharing is initiated through the app's available features.

We do not make your business records publicly available through this policy. Access depends on the app's account structure, assigned permissions, and sharing relationships. Review who has access before sharing business information.

We do not sell users' personal information to third parties. Information may be processed by the service providers described in this policy or disclosed where required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention and Backups

Information is retained as needed to provide the app's features and maintain your business records. The retention period may depend on the type of information, your use of the app, and the available backup and deletion features.

TuDoStock Book includes backup and recovery features for supported business records. Backup retention follows the backup settings and retention cycle provided by the app. Restoring records may add information to your business account according to the restore options you select.

7. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can manage supported business information, employee permissions, shared accounts, and account settings through the app.

TuDoStock Book provides a permanent account deletion feature. When a business owner confirms account deletion, the owner's account and associated business data, shop records, employee records, and backups are permanently deleted according to the app's deletion process.

Customer and supplier login accounts may remain active after the shop is deleted, but their access to that shop is removed. Information belonging independently to another account is not necessarily deleted when the owner's shop is deleted.

Before confirming deletion, review the in-app warning carefully, because this action cannot be undone. If you experience a problem with the deletion process, contact us at tudostocksupport@gmail.com.

You can also request account deletion without accessing the app by visiting our Account Deletion Request page . We will verify account ownership before processing the request.

8. Security

We use third-party cloud services and the app's available access controls to help protect information. Employee permissions and account sharing controls can help limit access to business records.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your sign-in credentials private, protect your device, and only grant access to people who need it. You are responsible for reviewing the access you grant to employees and shared-account recipients.

9. Children's Privacy

TuDoStock Book is designed for business and record-management purposes and is not intended to be directed at children. Do not provide children's personal information unless you are legally authorized to do so.

10. International Users

If you use TuDoStock Book from outside the country where our service providers operate, your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries. Data protection laws may differ between locations.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Please review this page periodically.
